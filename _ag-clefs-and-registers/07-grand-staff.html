Of the 4 common clefs, treble and bass clef are the ones that place the staff on the two more extreme registers. The piano, as well as other insturments with a wide range, uses something called a grand staff.

The grand staff consists of 2 staves joined together by braces a the start. Usually the top staff uses treble clef and the bottom uses bass clef. This allows us to write a very wide range by making used of both clefs.

At the lower end of the upper staff, or at the higher end of the lower staff, there is only the need for one ledger line, for writing middle C.

The grand staff is not just used for piano music. It is such a useful device that it is often used to write music for large ensembles, such as orchestras or choirs in a concise way.

8va signs

When the music goes so high that it becomes impractical to write even in treble clef, or too low even for bass clef, 8ve or 8va signs are used. This tells the performer that those notes are meant to be played and octave higher or lower than written.
