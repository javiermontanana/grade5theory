G F and C clefs

There are 3 kinds of clefs that we use: G clefs, C clefs and F clefs.

Each of these clefs is associated with a certain pitch, and by locating the clef on a certain staff-line, we are saying that that line corresponds to that particular pitch. From there follow the position of all the other pitches.

  • A G clef is associated with the G above middle C.
  • An F clef is associated with the F below middle C.
  • A C clef is associated with middle C.
  • The location of the G clef is the line that the clef starts from.
  • The location of the F clef is where the two dots are centred.
  • The location of the C clef is the centre of the clef.

In theory any clef can be located on any line, but there are 4 combinations that are most commonly used, and that you will see in the grade 5 theory exam. Later, when I refer to the 4 clefs, these are the clefs I'm refering to:

  • G clef on the 2nd line (known as treble clef)
  • F clef on the 4th line (known as bass clef)
  • C clef on the 3rd line (known as alto clef)
  • C clef on the 4th line (known as tenor clef)
