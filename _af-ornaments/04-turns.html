The precise performance of turns can vary greatly. There are certain conventions about how turns should be performed in the music of certain periods or composers, but in practice, a lot of the time a performer will decide on one or another interpretation according to their taste.

For the Grade 5 theory exam, you don't need to write out a way of performing a turn, but you must be able to spot one when shown it and not confuse it with any of the other ornaments. This should be easy if you remember that the turn goes once above and then once below the written note.
