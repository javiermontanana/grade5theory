--- layout: chapter title: Accidentals ---

In the previous examples, there were no accidentals, and because we transposed the key signature correctly, any sharps or flats required were taken care of by the key signature. To transpose examples with accidentals, there is nothing new you need to learn provided you know your intervals well.

In cases with accidentals it may be more useful to use methods 1 or 2 from earlier. It is likely that the notes with accidentals don't all fit nicely into a scale, so unless you're sure that method 3 is going to work well, it is best to use one of the others.
