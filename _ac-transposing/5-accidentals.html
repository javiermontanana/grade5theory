--- layout: chapter title: Accidentals ---

In the previous examples, there were no accidentals, and because we transposed the key signature correctly, any sharps or flats required were taken care of by the key signature. But you may have to transpose an example with accidentals. There is nothing really new or complicated about this, just transpose by the required interval.

In cases with accidentals it will be more useful to use methods 1 or 2 from earlier. It is likely that the notes with accidentals don't all fit nicely into a scale, so unless you're sure that method 3 is going to work well, it is best to use one of the others.
