# What is transposing

Transposing is simply moving a collection of notes up or down by a certain interval. 


simple examples, of 1 note, 2 notes....


# How to transpose

In order to be able to transpose, you need to be very confident with your intervals and keys and scales. The good news is that, as long as you're good at those, transposing is very easy.

## transposing the notes

3 ways:
- move each note by the transposing interval
- move the first note by the transposing interval, then follow the intervals in the melody. 
- work out the melody as scale degrees in the key, then the transposed meody will be the same scale degrees in the new key. (obviously keep it in the right register and octave)
