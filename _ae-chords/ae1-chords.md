# chords


## what are chords

construct chords

as stacked thirds
(major and minor thirds only)


So, for example, we could take the note C 
and add on top of it a third, E
and another third on top of that, G 
and we would then have a chord. 

aka triad, but i will use the word chord mostly.
-------------------------

## chord factors

As we have seen, we will add a third and another third. 

so the top note will be a fifth above the bottom note. 

we will call the notes root third and fifth. 

the root is the note chord is built on, but as we will see later, it doesn't have to appear at the bottom of the chord,
but the chord as a concept is created by taking the root and adding a third and a fifth on top. 
----------------------
