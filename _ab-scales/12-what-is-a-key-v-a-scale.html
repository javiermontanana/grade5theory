We have been using the words key and scale without properly defining them. In this section we will go deeper into what these words mean and properly disinguish betweeen the two.

A scale is the set of notes when it is ordered stepwise, ascending or descending. Key is the environment of the music, so that it will have the tonic as it's final arrival point. Music that is in a certain key will generally use notes form the scale(s) of that key, and for the purposes of the grade 5 exam, it is fine to think of keys in this way.

But as you learn more about how tonal music works, you'll understand that this is not strictly speaking true, and what really defines the key of the music is what note is the music directed towards so that it finds a sense of closure or resolution.

What we have done here is something called transposing. We have transposed the song up a major 2nd (major 2nd is the interval from G to A) which just means that we have moved the notes up by a major 2nd.
