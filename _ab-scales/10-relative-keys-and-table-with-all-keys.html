relative keys

As you can see, for each key signature, there is a major key and a minor key. We call keys which have the same key signature relative. A minor is the relative minor of C major, B♭ major is the relative major of G minor, etc. The relative major is always a minor 3rd above the relative minor.

The table of fifths including minor keys

Now that we have learnt all the major and minor keys, we can create a table that includes all of them. Like the one we made for the major keys, but also including minor keys. In this table, you move up a perfect fifth to go up, down a perfect fifth to go down. To go right (from relative major to minor) you move down a minor third and to go left (from relative minor to major) you move up a minor third.
