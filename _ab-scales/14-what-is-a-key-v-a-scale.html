We have been using the words key and scale almost interchangeably, but it is important to disinguishe betweeen the two.

A scale is the set of notes when it is ordered stepwise, ascending or descending. Key is the environment of the music, so that it will have the tonic as it's final arrival point. music that is in a certian key will use notes form the scale of the key, but will also have the tonic as the point of closure. as far as the grade 5 exam is concerned, you are absolutely fine to think of just the first feature of a key, but as you get more advanced, you will realise that this is not stricly speaking true and the second thing is really what defines the key of the music. related to cadences, chapter on chords.
