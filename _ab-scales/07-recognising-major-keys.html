What does it mean for music to be in a key. recognising keys

We say that a piece of music (or an extract of a piece) is in a certain key if it uses notes from that key or scale. For this you need to know your scales and key signatures very well, but the key signature is not always going to tell you what key you're in. You need to look at the notes that are sounded.
