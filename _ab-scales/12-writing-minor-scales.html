Writing minor scales (without key signatures)

You should be able to write any scale without using a key signature. When we learnt about major scales, we leanrt the pattern of tones and semitones.

I'd encourage you to think of 2 "halves" of the scale: note 1-5 note 5-8

notes 1-5 are always the same - whether it's natural melodic or harmonic, whether it's going up or down.
