Minor Scales

We have learnt about major scales. There is another kind of scale we need to learn about: minor sclaes.

Minor scales are a bit more complicated because there are 3 forms of the minor scale that we need to learn: natural, harmonic and melodic.

A minor natural melodic and harmonic

A minor is the key with no sharps or flats in the key signature. In this section we will learn the 3 forms of A minor: natural, melodi and harmonic. In the next section we wiil learn all the other keys and their key signatures.
