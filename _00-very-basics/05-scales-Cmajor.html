Scales

A scale is a set of notes ordered so that it goes up one step at a time, or down one step at a time. In the chapter on keys and scales you will learn about many different scales. But for now you should first know about the C major scale. The C major scale uses only natural notes (the white keys of the piano).

The C major Scale
