--- layout: chapter title: Compound Time Signatures ---

Compound time signatures are those with 6, 9, or 12 as the top number, but remember that they still have 2, 3, or 4 beats in a bar.

In compound time signatures, the top number is the number of subdivisions in the bar, not the number of beats. In compound time signatures, each beat divides naturally into 3 subdivisions, so:

  • when the top number is 6, there are 2 beats in each bar,
  • when the top number is 9, there are 3 beats in each bar,
  • when the top number is 12, there are 4 beats in each bar.

Let's look at an example:

In 6/8 there are 6 quavers in a bar. Remember that the quavers are not the beats, they are the subdivision. There are 2 beats in a bar. Each beat is 3 quavers long, so a dotted crotchet long.

In compound time signatures, the beats are always a dotted note value, which can divides naturally into 3 sibdivisions.
