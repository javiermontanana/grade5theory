--- layout: chapter title: Summary of Simple and Compound Time signatures ---

To summarise what we've learnt:

In both simple and compound time signatures there are 2, 3, or 4 beats in a bar.

In simple time signatures:

  • each beat is a non-dotted note value, which divides into 2 subdivisions
  • the top number is 2, 3, or 4 and this is the number of beats in a bar

In compound time signatures:

  • each beat is a dotted note value, which divides into 3 subdivisions
  • the top number is 6, 9, or 12 and this is the number of subdivisions in a bar
  • the number of beats in a bar is therefore the top number of the time signature divided by 3
