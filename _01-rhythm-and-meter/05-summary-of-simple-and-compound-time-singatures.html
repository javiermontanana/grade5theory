--- layout: chapter title: Summary of Simple and Compound Time signatures ---

To summarise what we've learnt:

In both simple and compound time signatures we have 2, 3, or 4 beats in a bar.

In simple time signatures each beat is a simple note value, and divides into 2 subdivisions.

In compound time signatures, each beat is a dotted note value, and divides into 3 subdivisions.

In simple time signatures, the top number will be 2, 3 or 4 and is the number of beats in a bar.

In compound time signatures, the top number will be 6, 9 or 12 and is the number of subdivisions (not beats) in a bar.
