--- layout: chapter title: Groupings --- beams: stick to one beam per beat. except in simple time, 2 beats ok 3 beats in 3 ok 2 beats in 4, for either half of the bar. Rests: in simple time: 1 rest per beat. except in 4ple, you can have 2 beat rest for either half off the bar. in compound: whole beats: 1 rest per beat. except in 4ple, you can have 2 beat rest for either half off the bar. fractions of beats: show subdivisions actually you can group subds 1 and 2 together.
