Basic Tempo Indications

The table below contains the most common tempo indications. They are roughly ordered from slow to fast.

In Italian, certain suffixes can be added to nouns and adjectives to denote an extreme form or a small form of them. The suffix -issimo implies an extreme form of the tempo and the suffixes -etto or -ino imply a smaller, lessened form.

We can then obtain the following terms:

  • prestissimo: faster than presto
  • allegretto: slower than allegro
  • adagietto: faster than adagio
  • larghetto: faster than largo
  • andantino: usually faster than andante, though sometimes slower

Other common tempo indications
