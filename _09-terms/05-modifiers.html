Connectors and modifiers are words that are used together with other terms to create more complex performance directions. In English, these would be words such as very, more, less, with, without, and, but, etc.

Modifiers

Modifiers change the degree, extent or intensity of another instruction.

Sudden or gradual change

These describe how quickly or gradually a change happens.

Italian term English meaning
subitosuddenly
poco a pocolittle by little; gradually

These are often seen complimenting dynamics, such as piano subito or crescendo poco a poco.

Connectors and linking words

These words combine other with other terms to form more complex phrases.

By themselves, these words are not meaningful performance directions, but they can be combined with other performance directions to create new, more precise ones, such as:

  • Poco più lento - a little slower
  • Allegro ma non troppo - fast, but not too much
  • Sempre legato - always smoothly
