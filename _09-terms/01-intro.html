In this chapter we will learn about the performance directions you need to know for the grade 5 theory exam. Performance directions are indications of how the music should be played. These may be related to tempo, dynamics, articulation, expression and other areas.

For historical reasons, in classical music, most performance directions are in Italian. In the 19th and 20th centuries it became more common for composers to use their own language.

Most of the terms that come into the grade 5 exam are in Italian. I have grouped these into several themes. There are also some terms in French and German in the final page of the chapter.
