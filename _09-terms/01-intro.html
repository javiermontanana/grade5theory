In this chapter we will learn about the performance directions you need to know for the grade 5 theory exam. Performance directions are indications of how the music should be played. These may be related to tempo, dynamics, articulation, expression and other areas.

For historical reasons, in classical music, most performance directions are in Italian. In the 19th and 20th centuries it became more common for composers to use their own language. In the grade 5 theory exam you will be tested on some Italian, French and German terms.
